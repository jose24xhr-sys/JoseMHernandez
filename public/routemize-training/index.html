Understand what Routemize does, how the software works, the main settings, and what happens from the moment a customer starts a booking until the appointment appears inside the platform.

  • AI-powered scheduling and route optimization.

  • Uses location, availability, service areas, working hours, and existing schedules.
  • Understand the core idea: Drive Less. Sell More.

Exercise 1: Booking & Scheduling · 60 minutes — Show exercise

Routemize Training — Exercise 1: Booking & Scheduling

Time Limit: 60 minutes

Training Type: Hands-on Practice & Feature Exploration

Objective: Understand Routemize's booking management, booking forms, scheduling settings, and route optimization capabilities.

Part 1 — Future Appointments (10 Minutes)

Navigate to Bookings → Future Appointments.

Explore the available actions for managing an existing appointment.

Questions:

  1. What happens when you select Adjust Duration? How does this affect the appointment and its scheduled time?
  1. What does Send Reschedule Link do? How does the customer interact with this link?
  1. What happens when you select Change Slots? How is this different from adjusting the duration?
  1. What is the difference between rescheduling an appointment internally and sending a reschedule link to a customer?
  1. In what situations would you recommend using each of these features?

Task: Test these actions on sample appointments and explain what you observed.

Part 2 — Create a Booking Form (15 Minutes)

Navigate to Booking Forms.

Imagine you are setting up Routemize for a company in an industry other than painting.

Examples include:

  • Landscaping
  • HVAC
  • Plumbing
  • Residential cleaning
  • Pest control
  • Roofing
  • Pool maintenance

Tasks:

  1. Choose a company name, industry, and service area.
  1. Create a new booking form for your company.
  1. Add at least three different services that your company offers.
  1. Configure different appointment durations for each service.
  1. Customize the booking form to collect relevant customer information.
  1. Review the booking form from the customer's perspective.

Questions:

  1. What is the purpose of having multiple services within a booking form?
  1. How does the selected service affect the booking process?
  1. What information should customers provide before scheduling?
  1. How would you share or embed this booking form on a company's website?

Note: You may use AI to research your chosen industry, create realistic services, and determine appropriate appointment durations.

Part 3 — Scheduling Settings (15 Minutes)

Explore the scheduling configuration and customize it for your fictional company.

A. Working Hours
  1. Configure your company's operating days.
  1. Set the start and end times for each working day.
  1. Include at least one day when the company is unavailable.

Question 10: How do working hours influence the appointment slots offered to customers?

B. Route Preferences

Navigate to Route Preferences.

Explore the available route optimization settings.

Tasks:

  1. Review the available route preferences.
  1. Choose the geographic area where your company operates.
  1. Configure your service area using one of the following methods:
  • Radius Map: Define a service radius around your company's location.
  • Custom Territory: Draw a specific geographic area where your company provides services.
  1. Save your configuration.

Questions:

  1. What is the difference between a radius-based service area and a custom territory?
  1. In what situations would you recommend using a custom territory instead of a radius?
  1. How do route preferences influence scheduling and appointment availability?
C. Holidays & Days Off

Tasks:

  1. Locate the holidays or days-off settings.
  1. Configure at least two unavailable dates.
  1. Review how those dates affect booking availability.

Question 14: What happens when a customer attempts to schedule an appointment on a day when the business is unavailable?

Part 4 — Create 15 Appointments (15 Minutes)

Now that your booking form, working hours, service area, and route preferences are configured, test your scheduling setup.

Tasks:

  1. Create a minimum of 15 appointments using realistic customer information and addresses.
  1. Distribute appointments across different locations within your selected service area.
  1. Use different services and appointment durations.
  1. Schedule appointments across multiple days.
  1. Review how Routemize organizes appointments.
  1. Explore the route and calendar views.

Questions:

  1. How does Routemize determine which appointment slots are available?
  1. What happens when two appointments are geographically close to each other?
  1. How does appointment location influence route efficiency?
  1. What happens when you attempt to schedule an appointment outside your configured service area?
  1. What differences do you notice between manually creating appointments and booking through the customer-facing form?

Important: Use fictional customer information and test appointments only. Do not send notifications to real customers.

Part 5 — Final Review (5 Minutes)

After completing the exercise, answer the following:

  1. Which Routemize feature did you find most useful, and why?
  1. Which feature was the most difficult to understand or configure?
  1. How would you explain Routemize's scheduling and route optimization capabilities to a new customer?
  1. If you were onboarding a new company, which settings would you configure first, and why?

Completion Checklist

Training Guidelines

You have one hour to complete this exercise.

You are encouraged to explore Routemize independently and use AI tools to research concepts or troubleshoot questions.

The goal is not simply to complete the configuration, but to understand what each feature does, how it affects scheduling, and when you would recommend it to a customer.

At the end of the session, be prepared to demonstrate your booking form, scheduling settings, service territory, and test appointments, and explain your answers.

Expected Result: A fully configured fictional company with a working booking form, scheduling preferences, service area, and at least 15 test appointments.

Exercise 2: Troubleshooting — Show questionnaire

Objective: Understand Google Calendar integration and troubleshoot customer scheduling issues in Routemize.

Part 1 — Google Calendar Playground

Before working on customer scenarios, explore the Google Calendar integration in Routemize.

Questions

  1. Why can multiple Google Calendars be synced to a Routemize account?
  2. What happens when you enable or disable Block Availability for a synced calendar?
  3. What is the difference between a Busy event and a Free event?
  4. How are Busy and Free events displayed in Routemize? Why might certain events not appear?
  5. What happens to existing calendar events after connecting or disconnecting a calendar?

Practical Scenario — Full-Day Block

Imagine you have a full-day event in Google Calendar that blocks your availability.

  1. Create a full-day Busy event in Google Calendar.
  2. Verify how it affects availability in Routemize.
  3. Temporarily disable availability blocking for that calendar.
  4. Book a test appointment during the previously blocked day.
  5. Enable availability blocking again.
  6. Verify what happens to the existing appointment and available time slots.

Final Question: Explain the complete process and how you would guide a customer through it.

Part 2 — Customer: Clearflow Plumbing

A customer has reported the following booking issues:

  1. Tuesday Availability: Why are Tuesdays not appearing on our booking form?
  2. Thursday Morning: Why can't customers book appointments at 6:00 AM or 7:00 AM on Thursdays?
  3. Friday Evening: Why can't customers book appointments starting at 6:00 PM on Fridays?
  4. Saturday Availability: Why aren't Saturdays available for booking, and how can we enable them?
  5. Monday the 16th: Mondays are available, but the 16th is showing as unavailable. Why?
  6. Friday the 27th: Why can't a customer book an appointment on Friday the 27th?
  7. Afternoon Availability: We work from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Why aren't appointments available after 2:00 PM?
  8. Occasional Saturdays: We occasionally perform estimates on Saturdays. What would you recommend to allow this without repeatedly changing our regular working hours?
  9. Rescheduling: Why can't I reschedule an appointment and assign it to Jose Ramirez?
  10. Team Member Availability: Why isn't availability showing when I try to book an appointment for Fresno Centeno?
  11. Booking Requests: When customers select "Something Else" on our booking form, why does it create a booking request instead of allowing them to schedule an appointment directly? How can we reduce friction and allow customers to self-book?

Recommended Areas to Investigate

  • Settings → Work Hours & Lunch
  • Settings → Route Preferences
  • Merge Assignment
  • Availability Settings
  • Booking Form → Scheduling & Confirmation Options
  • Google Calendar Integration

Part 3 — Training Instructions

These customer scenarios will be assigned through Intercom.

For each scenario:

  1. Respond: Reply as a Routemize Support Representative using your initial product knowledge.
  2. Investigate: Review the account configuration to identify the cause.
  3. Resolve: Modify settings when necessary, if permitted.
  4. Test: Verify the solution using a test customer booking whenever possible.
  5. Explain: Document the issue, its cause, and the solution provided.

Important: You may use AI and available support resources. The goal is to demonstrate your troubleshooting skills, understanding of Routemize, and ability to guide customers through scheduling issues.

Expected Result: Be prepared to explain your findings, demonstrate the Google Calendar scenario, and show how you resolved or investigated each Clearflow Plumbing issue.

Friday Exercise — Route Enhancement, Smart Field & Voice AI — Show exercise

Objective Explore route enhancement, Smart Field, customer notifications, and the Voice AI agent. Test each feature and explain what you observed, when it is useful, and how you would guide a customer.

Use a test account, fictional customer information, and phone numbers or email addresses you control. Review notification recipients before executing an action.

Part 1 — Route Enhancement

  1. Create multiple test appointments on the same day using different locations and appointment times.
  2. Review the initial route, appointment order, travel time, and available scheduling options.
  3. Find and review the route enhancement options. Record any requirements, choices, or confirmation messages.
  4. Execute the enhancement option on your test route and compare the result with the original route.
  5. Check how customers are notified when the option is executed. Review the trigger, recipients, channel, message content, and any customer action required using your test contact details.

Questions to answer

  1. What happens when you create multiple appointments on the same day?
  2. What changes after executing route enhancement? Compare appointment order, scheduled times, travel time, and route efficiency.
  3. When would this feature be useful, and what should a support representative check before recommending it?
  4. Are customer notifications automatic or optional? When are they sent, what information do they contain, and does the customer need to confirm anything?
  5. What happens if an appointment cannot be included or adjusted? Document any limitations or messages you encounter.

Part 2 — Smart Field

  1. Create test appointments on several future days with different locations and available time slots.
  2. Open the Smart Field view and explore its available options. Record how the appointments appear and which actions are available.
  3. Select an eligible test appointment and review the proposed action before executing it.
  4. Execute the option and compare the appointment, route, calendar, and availability before and after.
  5. Use your test contact details to check how customers are notified through the Smart Field flow and whether a response or confirmation is required.

Questions to answer

  1. What does Smart Field do with appointments scheduled on future days?
  2. Which appointments are eligible, and what determines the options shown?
  3. How would this feature help a customer manage appointments or improve scheduling?
  4. When you execute an option, who is notified, through which channel, and at what point? What does the customer see?
  5. What happens after the customer accepts, declines, or does not respond, if those options are available?
  6. How do the purpose, appointment changes, and notification flow compare with route enhancement?

If a notification or customer response cannot be tested, explain what you verified and what remains unconfirmed.

Part 3 — Review and Configure the Voice AI Agent

  1. Review the agent's current training, business information, instructions, and available settings.
  2. Identify information that is missing, unclear, duplicated, or out of date. Record the original configuration before making changes.
  3. Reorganize and improve the test agent's training and information. You may use AI to draft or fill in fictional business details, then review those details for accuracy and consistency.
  4. Explore the calling features, call history, call details, and any available recordings, transcripts, or summaries.
  5. Make test calls, review the results, and locate the call ID for each call.
  6. Send a test text message to the configured number. Check where it appears and whether it creates a request.

Questions to answer

  1. How can you find and copy the call ID for a specific call? Describe the exact steps and where the ID appears.
  2. What information can you review for a call, and how would you use it to investigate a customer issue?
  3. What happens when the number receives a text message? Where is it displayed?
  4. Does the text message appear as a request? If it does not, investigate why and explain the relevant behavior or configuration using evidence from your test.
  5. Which training or configuration changes did you make, and how did they affect the agent's responses?

Part 4 — Compare Expected and Actual Voice AI Responses

Run each scenario before and after updating the test agent's training. Write the expected answer based on the information the agent has, then compare it with the actual response.

  1. Business information test Ask, “What services do you offer, which areas do you serve, and what are your working hours?” Does the answer match the existing business information and your updated training?
  2. Scheduling test Ask for a service or appointment outside the configured hours or service area. Does the agent follow the rules you provided, explain the limitation, and offer an appropriate next step?
  3. Unknown information test Ask a question whose answer is absent from the training. Does the agent handle the uncertainty appropriately and follow any escalation instructions?
  4. Follow-up test Change a detail during the conversation or ask a follow-up question. Does the agent retain the correct context and respond consistently?

Comparison questions

  • How did the original agent's response compare with the response expected from its existing information?
  • After your training changes, did the agent respond as expected? Which specific instruction or business detail supports your assessment?
  • Did you find any incorrect answers, missing information, or inconsistent behavior? What did you change, and what happened when you repeated the test?

Final Findings

Prepare a short demonstration and findings report covering your test setup, steps, expected results, actual results, and supporting screenshots or call IDs.

  • Show the before-and-after route enhancement results.
  • Demonstrate Smart Field with future appointments and explain when it is useful.
  • Explain and compare the customer notification flows for both features.
  • Show your Voice AI training changes, test calls, call IDs, and text-message findings.
  • Compare expected and actual agent responses and list any unresolved questions.
