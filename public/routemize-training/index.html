What is Routemize?

Routemize is an AI-powered scheduling and route optimization platform built for field service businesses — especially appointment-driven contractors like painting, epoxy flooring, concrete resurfacing, and similar trades.

Instead of manually juggling calendars and driving all over town, Routemize automatically places appointments in the smartest geographic order, saving your team time and fuel every day.

📍
Drive Less. Sell More.

Routemize considers your team's location, availability, and service area when scheduling — so every day's route is optimized before your estimator leaves the driveway.

Creating your account

To get started, open Google Chrome and go to:

ids.routemize.com

  1. Click Get Started Free.

  2. Click Sign Up with Google and select your Google account. This is the fastest method and skips manual email verification.

  3. If a browser security prompt appears, click Advanced → Go to Routemize → Continue and check the permission checkbox.

  4. Fill out your company information on the registration form.

  5. Select your plan (see below).

  6. Click Complete Registration.

✅
Use Google Chrome

Routemize works best in Google Chrome. Using Safari or other browsers during setup can cause slowness or display issues.

Choosing the right plan

Routemize has three plans based on how many users (team members) you need:

Plan Users Included Best For
Solo Starter 1 user Owner-operators running their own estimates
Pro Most Popular 2 users Owner + 1 estimator or field rep
Scale Teams 3+ users Multi-estimator teams
⚠️
Upgrade before adding team members

If you're on the Solo plan and try to add a second user, you'll see a billing prompt. Go to Settings → Your Plan, click Pro Monthly, then Switch to Pro, and refresh the page before proceeding.

Setting up your branding

After registration, the onboarding wizard walks you through branding. This controls how your booking form looks to customers.

  1. In the wizard, click Branding.

  2. Click Autofill from Website — Routemize will pull your logo and suggest brand colors automatically from your website.

  3. Confirm or adjust up to 3 brand colors.

  4. Click Apply Colors, then scroll down and click Continue.

🎨
Tweak it later

You can always return to Settings → Branding to update your logo or colors after setup.

Configuring work hours

Routemize uses your work hours to know when appointments can be scheduled. Set these to reflect when your estimator is actually available in the field.

  1. Select Home as the starting location (Routemize calculates routes from where your team member starts each day).

  2. Set your working days and hours. If your estimator works Saturdays, make sure to enable Saturday.

  3. For the appointment schedule, configure:

    • Leave at: Work Start Time
    • First appointment: Your preferred start (e.g., 8:00 AM)
    • Last appointment ends: End at Work Hours

  4. Click Continue.

Profile setup

On the Profile screen, enter your first name, last name, and mobile phone number. Scroll down to confirm your home address is pre-filled correctly, then click Continue.

Connecting Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar allows Routemize to read your existing events and automatically schedule new appointments around them — so nothing gets double-booked.

  1. When prompted in the wizard, click Connect Google Calendar.

  2. Select the correct Google account and authorize access.

  3. Click Continue, then Get Started.

⚠️
Using DripJobs? Read this first.

If your DripJobs jobs are syncing to Google Calendar, Routemize will see those as "Busy" blocks and may show very limited availability. Before going live, either disable the Google Calendar sync in DripJobs (Settings → My Profile → toggle off Sync Jobs to Calendar → Save), or manually change past job events in Google Calendar from Busy to Free. See the DripJobs Integration article for full details.

Next steps

Once your account is set up, here's what to do next:

  • 🗺️ Add your service area — draw the geographic zone where you accept appointments
  • 📋 Create booking forms — build the scheduling links you'll share with customers and put on your website
  • 👥 Add team members — if you have additional estimators or field reps
  • 📱 Install the mobile app — so your field team can manage their day from their phone
